Common questions

Does Budgety connect to my bank?

No. You enter your balances yourself. Budgety never asks for banking credentials and has no connection to any financial institution — that is deliberate, and it is why the app can promise that nothing leaves your phone.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

I deleted the app and lost everything. Can you restore it?

Unfortunately not. Because we never receive a copy of your information, there is nothing on our side to restore from. If your figures are important, keep a note of them somewhere else as well.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts consecutive days on which you recorded at least one contribution. Miss a day and it resets to zero. Streaks of 3, 7, 30 and 100 days each unlock a badge.

How do I earn badges?

There are eight. Four come from streaks, and four from filling a pod to 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%. You can see which ones you have on the Profile screen.

Can I change my currency?

Yes — Profile, then Currency. This changes the symbol shown throughout the app. It does not convert existing amounts, so pick the currency your figures are already in.

Does Budgety cost anything?

No. This version is completely free, with no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Found a bug?

Tell us what happened, what you expected instead, and which iPhone and iOS version you are on. That is usually enough for us to reproduce it.
