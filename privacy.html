Budgety

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Budgety does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no server to sync with, and no analytics running in the background. Everything you enter stays in the app's storage on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Budgety has no backend. We do not operate servers that receive, store or process your information, and we have no way to see what you enter into the app.

What stays on your device

The information you type into Budgety is saved locally on your iPhone so the app can show it back to you the next time you open it. This includes:

  • The name you enter during setup
  • Account names, types, balances and amounts owed
  • Savings pod names, targets and progress
  • Contributions you record, including any notes and dates
  • Badges you earn and your contribution streak
  • Your currency and reminder preferences

This information never leaves your device through Budgety. It is not uploaded, backed up to our systems, shared, sold or used for advertising.

No bank connections

Budgety does not connect to banks or financial institutions. There is no Plaid integration or equivalent. We never ask for banking credentials, card numbers or account numbers, and the balances you enter are figures you type in yourself — not live data pulled from anywhere.

No tracking, no ads

Budgety contains no analytics tools, no advertising networks and no third-party tracking software. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers or device fingerprinting, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups enabled on your iPhone, iOS may include Budgety's stored information in those backups. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms — we have no access to it. You can manage or disable this in your iPhone's Settings.

Information Apple may receive

If you download Budgety from the App Store, Apple handles that transaction and applies its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share crash and usage data with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. These reports contain technical diagnostics only — never the financial information you keep in the app.

Links that open outside the app

This policy and our Terms of Use open in a web browser from inside Budgety. Visiting them is a normal web request, and the host of those pages may keep standard server logs. Budgety itself sends nothing along with the request.

Deleting your information

You are always in control:

  • Erase all data in the app's Profile screen permanently deletes everything Budgety has stored, immediately.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its storage along with it.

Because we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Budgety is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since the app collects nothing at all, no personal information from a child can reach us.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. Budgety holds none, so these rights have nothing to attach to. Your information is already entirely in your hands, on your own device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Budgety introduces something that changes how information is handled — optional cloud sync, subscriptions or advertising, for example — we will update this page and change the date above before that version ships. Material changes will also be described in the App Store release notes.
